# KBC Lucky Draw & WhatsApp Lottery Advance Fee Fraud

1. **Bogus Winnings**: Unsolicited letters or audio messages claiming you won ₹25,00,000 in a "WhatsApp Lucky Draw" or "Kaun Banega Crorepati Official Sweepstakes".
2. **Advance Processing Fee**: To release the cheque or bank transfer, victim is told to pay "tax", "RBI registration fee", or "custom clearance" amounting to ₹15,000–₹50,000.
3. **Core Rule**: You can never win a lottery you did not purchase tickets for. Legitimate lotteries never ask winners to deposit advance cash fees into personal savings bank accounts.
4. **Action**: Block the sender number, report the chat on WhatsApp, and do not forward the message to friends or family.
