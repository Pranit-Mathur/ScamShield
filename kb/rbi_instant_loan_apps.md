# Illegal Instant Loan Apps & Extortion Rackets

1. **Unregulated APKs**: Unverified loan apps offer collateral-free loans within 2 minutes without credit bureau checks, asking for full phone permissions: Contacts, Gallery, Location, and Call Logs.
2. **Deduction and Extortion**: Scammers disburse only ₹2,500 on a ₹5,000 sanctioned loan and demand ₹8,000 back within 6 days. When payments lapse, morph photos and contact list harassment ensue.
3. **Safe Steps**:
   - Only borrow from RBI-registered Banks and NBFCs. Check the Sachet portal (sachet.rbi.org.in).
   - Never allow Gallery or Contact permissions to lending apps.
   - If trapped, file a complaint on 1930 and with local police cyber crime unit immediately.
