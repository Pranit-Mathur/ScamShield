# CERT-In Alert: Fraudulent Customer Support Toll-Free Numbers

Cybercriminals create fake websites and alter Google Business listings for airline refunds, e-commerce support, electricity bill payments, and couriers with mobile numbers posing as toll-free helpdesks:

1. **Verify Numbers Only on Verified Official Apps**: Do not call phone numbers discovered through random search engine queries or Google Maps listings. Always access customer service through the verified official application.
2. **Beware of Requests to Initiate ₹1 or ₹5 Test Transactions**: Scammers ask victims to transfer nominal amounts to "verify the payment gateway" or "link refund wallet", capturing user details or triggering recurring autopay mandates.
3. **Never Share OTP or Forward SMS**: Support executives will never ask you to forward incoming SMS messages or provide two-factor authorization codes.
