# RBI Warning on Fake KYC Updation / SIM Expiry SMS

Reserve Bank of India reiterates that banks never send SMS or WhatsApp messages containing external APK links, Bitly URLs, or 10-digit mobile numbers demanding immediate KYC verification.

1. **Never Click Unverified Links**: Official bank communications originate from authorized alphanumeric Sender IDs (e.g., `AD-HDFCBK`, `VK-SBIINB`), never standard +91 mobile numbers.
2. **Never Install Remote Screen-Sharing Apps**: Fraudsters coerce victims into downloading apps like AnyDesk, TeamViewer, RustDesk, or malicious APKs masked as "SBI Quick Support" or "KYC Tool". These grant attackers full remote viewing and OTP theft capability.
3. **Banks Never Ask for PIN or Password**: Periodic KYC updates must always be done via your official netbanking portal or by visiting your home branch in person.
4. **Immediate Redressal**: If you shared confidential credentials, change your login password and UPI PIN immediately and report to your bank's fraud monitoring cell.
