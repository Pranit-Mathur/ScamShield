# Electricity Disconnection & Postal Delivery SMS Traps

1. **Urgent Disconnection Warning**: "Dear consumer, your electricity power will be disconnected tonight at 9:30 PM because previous month bill was not updated. Immediately contact Electricity Officer on 98xxxxxxxx."
2. **India Post Parcel Delivery Warning**: "Your package IND8492048 cannot be delivered due to incomplete address. Update your address within 12 hours at [phishing link] or item will be returned."
3. **Detection Signals**:
   - Power distribution discoms (like Tata Power, BESCOM, BSES, MSEDCL) never notify disconnection via personal mobile numbers.
   - Genuine India Post tracking is strictly done at indiapost.gov.in, not on third-party domains like `indiapost-update.xyz`.
4. **Protective Measures**: Never pay through links sent over SMS. Use official municipal or utility payment gateways or established bank bill-pay sections.
