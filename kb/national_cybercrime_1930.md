# National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal - 1930 Helpline & Golden Hour Protocol

If you have been defrauded or sent money under duress or deception, act within the first **2 hours ("Golden Hour")**:

1. **Call 1930 immediately**: The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal helpline coordinates with bank nodal officers and NPCI to freeze funds along the mule beneficiary chain before cash-out.
2. **Lodge an Incident on cybercrime.gov.in**: Note the 14-digit acknowledgement number, transaction reference (UTR / UPI Ref ID), recipient VPA/account number, and time stamp.
3. **Notify Your Bank to Block UPI & Card Access**: Contact your debiting bank's 24x7 emergency helpline to freeze the account or hotlist your cards, preventing recurring unauthorized mandates.
4. **Preserve Digital Evidence**: Screenshot WhatsApp/SMS threads, incoming caller IDs, fake APK download links, and payment confirmation screens without deleting messages.
