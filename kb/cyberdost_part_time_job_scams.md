# Ministry of Home Affairs: Modus Operandi of Part-Time Job / Task Investment Fraud

The Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) highlights rampant Telegram task frauds promising ₹2,000 to ₹5,000 daily for liking YouTube videos, rating Google Maps hotels, or reviewing movies:

1. **The Bait Phase**: Victims are paid small legitimate sums (₹150 to ₹500) into their UPI accounts to establish fake trust and build confidence.
2. **The "Prepaid Merchant Task" Trap**: Victims are added to high-engagement Telegram groups with fake participants sharing forged profit screenshots, and instructed to deposit ₹5,000, ₹20,000, or ₹1,00,000 on bogus crypto/merchant dashboards.
3. **Refusal to Withdraw**: When the victim requests a withdrawal, fraudsters claim the account is "frozen for KYC error" or demand a 30% "processing tax" to release funds.
4. **Actionable Rule**: Genuine corporate recruiters never recruit through unsolicited WhatsApp messages, never use Telegram channels as official offices, and never ask job seekers to pay upfront money for tasks.
