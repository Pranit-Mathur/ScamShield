# Law Enforcement Advisory: The "Digital Arrest" & Fake Police Scam

High-level advisory issued by CBI, ED, and Mumbai Police regarding intimidation scams where fraudsters pose as police officers, customs officials, or telecom regulators:

1. **No Legal Provision for 'Digital Arrest'**: Law enforcement agencies do not conduct interrogations via Skype, WhatsApp video call, or MS Teams, and will NEVER place a citizen under "digital arrest" inside their house.
2. **Fabricated Narcotics / Money Laundering Claims**: Fraudsters claim an intercepted package in Mumbai / Taiwan contained narcotics, passports, and illicit SIM cards registered in your name.
3. **Coerced Transfer to 'RBI Secret Verification Account'**: Victims are pressured into liquidating fixed deposits and transferring funds to "clear their name" into mule accounts labeled as official reserve escrow accounts.
4. **Immediate Redressal**: Cut the call immediately. Report to local cyber cell and dial 1930. Genuine police summons are served in person through official postal or authorized channels.
