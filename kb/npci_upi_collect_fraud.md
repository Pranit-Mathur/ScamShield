# NPCI Safe UPI Guidelines: Collect Requests & Scanning QR Codes

National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) warns users regarding deceptive collect requests and QR codes disguised as incoming refunds or cashback:

1. **Entering UPI PIN Always Deducts Money**: Remember the golden rule of UPI: You **NEVER** need to enter your UPI PIN or scan a QR code to receive money. PIN is required only when transferring money out.
2. **Deceptive Collect Requests**: Scammers initiate payment requests with titles like "REFUND RECEIVED - Rs 5,000" or "OLX BUYER APPROVAL". Approving this prompt debit money from your account.
3. **Verify Payee UPI ID (VPA)**: Always verify the actual handle before authorization. Fraudsters use lookalike VPAs such as `paytm-refund-approval@icici` or `phonepe-support-desk@ybl` which are personal accounts rather than official corporate settlement handles.
4. **Decline and Block**: Immediately reject any unexpected collect request on Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM and mark the handle as fraudulent.
